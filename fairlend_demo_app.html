Credit default prediction under fairness constraints for micro-finance lending

BTECH CAP 501, Project Life Cycle Management, 2026-27. uGDX School of Technology, ATLAS SkillTech University.

Problem

Thin-file borrowers are scored on alternative data such as recharges, mobile money and utility bills. That data carries historical bias, so an accurate model can still deny women and rural regions more often. Removing gender from the inputs does not fix it, because other features act as proxies.

Approach

  • Binary classifier for default (90+ days past due).
  • Pre-processing: reweighing by gender and region.
  • Post-processing: group-specific thresholds.
  • Pareto view to choose a model, with a production gate at DIR ≥ 0.80.
  • Three plain-language reason codes per score, and human review within ±5 points of the cut-off.
